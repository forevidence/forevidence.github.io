Neutrality and Independence Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective July 30, 2026 · Next scheduled review July 30, 2027

1. Why this policy exists

ForEvidence produces evaluations, assurance artifacts, and public information about AI systems used in regulated settings. The value of that work rests entirely on one property: that our findings are not shaped by the interests of the parties whose systems we assess.

Independence is not a disposition. It is a structure. This policy sets out the structure — what we will and will not do, who decides, and how anyone can check.

We publish it because an assurance provider that asks others to make verifiable claims must make verifiable claims itself.

2. Scope

This policy binds ForEvidence LLC, its member, employees, contractors, advisors, and any entity it controls. It applies to all work products, including:

  • the public index and any free information service we operate;
  • paid evaluations, audits, readiness assessments, and assurance reports;
  • Provenance Seal artifacts and any attestation bearing the ForEvidence name;
  • written commentary, standards contributions, and public submissions.

3. Three roles, and one that is closed

Every organization we encounter occupies one or more of three roles:

RoleDefinitionPermitted?
SupplierProvides tools, infrastructure, or models that ForEvidence uses in its own operationsYes, with disclosure
SubjectDevelops or deploys an AI system that ForEvidence evaluates or indexesYes
ClientCommissions and pays for an evaluation of its own systemsYes, with the controls in §6
GovernorHolds a seat, vote, veto, or advisory role over ForEvidence's methods, criteria, ranking, scope, or findingsNo — closed to all subjects and suppliers

The governor role is closed without exception. No AI developer, model provider, cloud provider, EHR vendor, or deployer of a system within our assessment scope may hold a board seat, advisory seat, steering-committee position, methodology vote, or any other channel of influence over what we measure or what we publish.

This is a deliberate design choice informed by the record of multi-stakeholder health-AI bodies whose credibility was contested precisely because major vendors sat in governance rather than participating as suppliers of information. Participation is welcome. Governance is not available.

4. What we will not accept

ForEvidence does not and will not:

  1. Accept payment for placement, ranking, inclusion, or favorable characterization in the public index or any published material. Index inclusion is never purchased and never withheld for non-payment.
  2. Grant subjects pre-publication approval rights. Subjects receive a factual-accuracy review window (§7); they do not receive editorial control, and we publish over objection where we judge the finding correct.
  3. Hold equity, options, revenue share, or carried interest in any organization that is a subject of our assessments, or accept such instruments as consideration.
  4. Contingent-fee arrangements. Fees are never tied to the outcome, rating, or conclusion of an assessment.
  5. Serve as both designer and assessor of the same control. Where we have advised on the design of a system, governance program, or control, we do not subsequently issue an independent assessment of that same artifact.
  6. Permit any single client to exceed 25% of annual revenue without disclosure and a documented independence review; sustained concentration above that level triggers either divestment of the engagement or public notation on all affected work products.

5. Vendor programs, certifications, and marketing relationships

We recognize that model providers and platform vendors operate partner programs, certification tracks, directories, and co-marketing arrangements. These confer real technical benefits and real reputational entanglement.

Our rule is symmetry and separation:

  • Individual credentials are permitted. Personnel may hold vendor-issued technical certifications, which attach to the individual and reflect competence, not endorsement. These are disclosed.
  • Firm-level partner or directory listings are not held with any provider whose systems fall within our assessment scope, unless the same status is (a) sought and disclosed across all comparable providers within scope, and (b) carries no marketing, referral, revenue, or lead-flow obligation running in either direction.
  • We do not accept co-marketing funds, market-development funds, referral fees, or joint sales enablement from any subject or supplier.
  • We do not describe ourselves as a partner of, or certified by, any AI provider in connection with assurance work.

Where a technical relationship is necessary — for example, because we use a provider's models in our own tooling — that provider is recorded as a supplier in the register (§8), and the relationship is procurement, not partnership.

6. Structural separation of advisory and assurance

ForEvidence may, over time, offer both (a) independent assurance and (b) technical or governance advisory services. These are separated as follows:

  • Advisory and assurance engagements for the same client on the same subject matter are not accepted concurrently, and a cooling-off period of 12 months applies in either direction.
  • Personnel who deliver advisory work on a given system are recused from assessing it.
  • Should the two lines grow beyond what a single entity can credibly separate, the assurance function will be constituted as a distinct entity with its own governance, and this policy will be amended accordingly.

Until such time, the sole member of ForEvidence LLC is the accountable decision-maker for all independence determinations, and every determination is logged.

7. Methodological independence

  • Assessment criteria are derived from published, externally maintained references — including the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO 14971, the EU AI Act, and applicable sectoral regulation — and from our own published methodology.
  • We will help clients to implement their own evaluations separately, independent of the verification-related parts.
  • Where a criterion derives from binding law, and where it derives from non-binding guidance, we state which, because the distinction changes what a finding means.
  • Methodology changes are versioned, dated, and published before they are applied.
  • Subjects receive a defined window to identify factual errors before publication. Corrections of fact are made and logged. Disagreements of judgment are recorded alongside the finding, not resolved in the subject's favor by default.
  • Every published finding carries provenance: the evidence relied on, the date assessed, the version of the methodology applied, and the system version in scope.

8. Public disclosure register

ForEvidence maintains a public register at forevidence.ai/register, updated within 30 days of any change, recording:

  • all suppliers whose products are used in ForEvidence's assessment tooling;
  • all vendor programs, certifications, and directory listings held at firm or individual level;
  • all recusals and independence determinations, in summary form.

Client identities are disclosed where the client consents or where a work product is published; the existence of confidential engagements and their aggregate share of revenue is disclosed regardless.

9. Recusal

Any person aware of a circumstance that a reasonable external observer might regard as compromising to independence must declare it before work begins. The default response is recusal. Where recusal is not practicable, the circumstance is disclosed on the face of the work product, and the affected party may decline the engagement.

The test is not whether we believe we can remain objective. It is whether an informed outsider would have grounds to doubt it.

10. Challenge and complaint

Any person may challenge a published finding, a methodology, or an independence determination by writing to info@forevidence.ai.

  • Acknowledgement within 5 business days.
  • Substantive response within 30 days.
  • Substantiated challenges result in correction, and the correction is published with its date and reason; the original is not silently removed.
  • Complaint volume and disposition are reported in summary in the annual transparency note.

11. Amendment

This policy may be amended only by the accountable decision-maker, in writing, with the change, its rationale, and its effective date published. Superseded versions remain publicly accessible. No amendment applies retroactively to work already published.

ForEvidence LLC is a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of New York. This policy is a statement of operating commitments; it does not create third-party contractual rights.
